\section{Introduction}
We consider a driven-dissipative cavity coupled to an ensemble of three-level atoms for generating spin squeezing through a Berry phase that depends on the active atom number. We use an effective spin model obtained by adiabatically eliminating the cavity in the bad-cavity limit. Each atom has two long-lived states, $\ket{\up}$ and $\ket{\down}$, and an excited state $\ket{e}$. The drive couples $\ket{\down} \leftrightarrow \ket{e}$, while $\ket{\up}$ is uncoupled. Therefore, for a fixed number of atoms in the active manifold,
\begin{equation}
    \Nj = N_\down + N_e,
\end{equation}
the dynamics reduce to an effective two-level system in the $\{\ket{\down},\ket{e}\}$ manifold.
\par

Due to the resulting strong symmetry, a pure state in the case of homogeneous atom-drive coupling may be decomposed into subspaces with a fixed number of atoms in the active manifold, $\Nj$, as
\begin{equation}
    \ket{\psi(t)}
    = \sum_{N_J=0}^{N}
    c_{N_J}(t)\,\ket{\psi_{N_J}(t)},
    \qquad
    \sum_{N_J=0}^{N} |c_{N_J}(t)|^2 = 1.
\end{equation}
The coherent dynamics within each $\Nj$ subspace are governed, in the rotating frame of the drive, by the active-manifold Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
    \hat H_\delta = |\Omega| \hat J_x - \delta \hat N_e,
\end{equation}
where $\Omega$ is the effective Rabi frequency, $\delta$ is the detuning of the drive, and the excited-state number operator $\hat N_e$ and collective spin operator $\hat J_x$ are defined as
\begin{equation}
    \hat N_e = \sum_{i=1}^{N_J} \ket{e}\bra{e}_i,
    \qquad
    \hat J_x = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N_J} \left(\ket{\down}\bra{e}_i + \ket{e}\bra{\down}_i\right).
\end{equation}
\par

In the case of two distinct atom-drive coupling strengths, specified by the Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
    \hat H_\delta = |\Omega| \big( \omega_1 \hat J_{1,x}
    + \omega_2 \hat J_{2,x} \big)
    - \delta \hat N_e,
\end{equation}
where $\omega_1 + \omega_2 = 2$, a pure state may similarly be decomposed into subspaces with fixed numbers of atoms in the active manifold for each coupling strength, $\Nj = N_{J_1} + N_{J_2}$, as
\begin{equation}
    \ket{\psi(t)}
    = \sum_{N_J=0}^{N}
    \sum_{N_{J_1}=0}^{N_J}
    c_{N_J,N_{J_1}}(t)\,\ket{\psi_{N_J,N_{J_1}}(t)},
    \qquad
    \sum_{N_J=0}^{N} \sum_{N_{J_1}=0}^{N_J} |c_{N_J,N_{J_1}}(t)|^2 = 1.
\end{equation}
\par

The three-stage protocol is implemented as
\begin{equation}
    \left\{
    \begin{array}{lll}
        \text{Phase 1:} & \Omega(t)=\Omega_0, & \delta(t)=0,        \\
        \text{Phase 2:} & \Omega(t)=\Omega_0, & \delta(t)=\delta_0, \\
        \text{Phase 3:} & \Omega(t)=0,        & \delta(t)=0.
    \end{array}
    \right.
\end{equation}
\par

Phase 1 prepares the dissipative steady state in the active manifold. Phase 2 applies a detuning and accumulates a sector-dependent Berry phase. Phase 3 turns off the drive and allows the excited population to decay back to $\ket{\down}$.
